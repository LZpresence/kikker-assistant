# Kikker Assistant

Kikker the frog keeps your day on track: a weather-aware morning (bike at sunrise, or karate / a workout),
the after-work must-dos, a project hour, your daughter's bedtime routine, wind-down and bedtime, plus weekly goals
across physical, mental, family & social, and spiritual health. Everything is stored on the phone.

- Web app: https://lzpresence.github.io/kikker-assistant/
- Android app (reminders): https://lzpresence.github.io/kikker-assistant/download/
