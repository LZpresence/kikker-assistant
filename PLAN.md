# Kikker Assistant plan

## Phase 1: daily rhythm (built)
- Weekday and weekend routines with editable times and checklists
- Morning pick from the forecast: wake 10 min before sunrise for a bike ride when it's dry, mild and calm and the sun is up by the end of the wake window; otherwise wake at the start of the window for karate or a home workout (whichever was done less recently)
- After-work checklist, daughter's bedtime routine, project hour with a rotating project and its next small step, 1-hour timer and log
- Wind-down check-out: mood, what went well, one thing for tomorrow, in bed on time; tomorrow's plan with a one-tap phone alarm (Android)
- Weekly goals by area, counted from what you log
- Android app: native reminders for each part of the day, scheduled 7 days ahead
- Data on the phone only, with copy/paste backup

## Phase 2: morning briefing
- A scheduled Claude routine reads work email and Teams (Microsoft 365), Slack, and personal email, writes a short briefing (needs a reply / today / can wait) and delivers it privately to the app
- Later: text messages

## Phase 3
- Sunday weekly review by area with one focus for next week
- Calendar link so Kikker knows when the workday actually ends
- Home-screen widget showing what's next
